📱 Phone Sales & Ads Analysis

📊 Project Overview: This project analyzes the quarterly sales performance of two smartphone brands, alpha and nova, in relation to advertising spend.

📈 Bar Plot: Visualizes units sold per quarter for each brand, showing trends and differences between brands.

💰 Scatter Plot: Examines the relationship between ad spend and units sold, helping to understand marketing effectiveness.

🟢 Line Plot: Tracks units sold across quarters, providing a clear view of sales trends over time.

📉 Histogram: Shows the distribution of units sold, highlighting the frequency of different sales levels.

🧮 Summary Statistics: Provides min, max, median, mean, and quartiles of units sold to give a quick numeric overview.

🔍 Linear Regression Model: Predicts units sold based on advertising spend, allowing estimation of future sales. For example, predicting units sold for an ad spend of 45.

🚀 Insights: Brand alpha generally sells more units than nova, and higher ad spend correlates with higher sales.

🌟 Purpose: Demonstrates the use of R for data visualization, statistical summaries, and predictive modeling in a marketing context.
